FAQ

What every owner asks before starting.

How do you measure success?

Conversions — inquiries, bookings, closed revenue — tracked on a client-facing dashboard from day one. Views and followers are instruments, never the score.

Is the content labeled as AI?

Yes, wherever platform policy requires it — and the data shows disclosure doesn't suppress performance; low-quality content does. Our persona talent is AI-transparent by design. What converts is craft and story.

What happens if we part ways?

Everything keeps running. Infrastructure lives on your servers, with your API keys and your data. You lose the ongoing optimization — never the machine.

Why is the diagnostic paid?

Because a serious diagnostic takes serious work: full operations mapping, an eight-variable analysis, and ROI on your real numbers. It's also credited toward your engagement when you proceed.
