FAQ

What every owner asks before starting.

What happens if I cancel maintenance?

Everything keeps running. The systems live on your server, with your API keys and your data. Canceling ends the ongoing optimization and priority support — never the infrastructure.

Why charge for the consulting instead of giving it away?

Because a serious diagnostic takes serious work: full operations mapping, an 8-variable analysis, and ROI on your real numbers. And 50% is credited toward your build if you proceed.

Does it work for my industry?

The methodology is framework-based, not template-based — it applies to any business whose operations can be mapped. For several industries, templated solutions already accelerate the build.

What does it cost to operate the systems?

Infrastructure costs (hosting and APIs, typically $1,275–3,536 MXN/mo) are paid by you directly to the providers. AELION doesn't resell or mark up your infrastructure.
